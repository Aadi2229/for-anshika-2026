Chapter one of forever

Our Love Story

It began at Navyug School, Mandir Marg.

Two very different students

We both joined the school in the same year, but in different classes. I was in Class 11th, and she was in Class 9th. And honestly, we couldn't have been more different.

I was the guy who loved being on stage—always ready for debates, speeches, presentations, and basically any opportunity to speak in front of a crowd.

And then there was her. The prettiest, shyest, and most beautiful girl I had ever seen.

6th December

I was presenting one of my research-based projects on the “Silver Ratio.” I was confidently explaining my project when I noticed a girl sitting there.

a number, and then something more beautiful
Silver RatioGolden Ratio
2.414

And suddenly, I discovered something more beautiful than the Silver Ratio.

She was my Golden Ratio. ✨

Funny enough, the most confident boy in school—who could speak in front of hundreds of people without hesitation—couldn't speak a single word to her.

The dance

For the next few days, I didn't see her. Then, during one of the school functions, she had a dance performance.

I had never really been interested in dance before. But that day, somehow, I discovered that dance could actually be beautiful—and incredibly attractive—especially when she was the one dancing. ❤️

Winter break

And just when I thought I might finally get a chance to see her again… winter break started.

So, during the holidays, I did what any completely-normal-and-definitely-not-in-love boy would do. I tried my best to find out which class she was in, what her name was, and everything else I could possibly find out. 😂

My legendary first move

Eventually, I gathered enough courage to approach her. And my legendary first move was:

Would you like to be my friend? Hehehe. 😂❤️

Any excuse to see her

And then began a phase where I didn't really need any reason to go to her class. I just wanted an excuse to see her face.

I'd stand in the corridor, hoping to catch just one glimpse of her beautiful face. One look was enough to make my day.

The confession

And then came the moment when I finally decided to confess my feelings. I proposed to her, but with one very important request:

If you wanna say no, please don't tell this to anyone. 😂

Valentine's Day

And then came Valentine's Day. The day she finally said yes.

Yes ❤️

And that's how it all started.

From two students in different classes at Navyug School…

To two people who somehow found each other.

From a boy who could speak confidently on any stage…

To the same boy who couldn't even say a proper word to the girl he liked.

From the Silver Ratio…

To finding his Golden Ratio.

And today, we're still here. Still together. Still making memories. Still growing together.

And hopefully, this isn't just a love story that started at Navyug School. It's a love story we're going to make last forever. ❤️

And this is just the beginning.
